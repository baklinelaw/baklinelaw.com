§ 1Practice

Practice areas

The firm takes a limited number of matters so that each one is prepared as if it will be tried.

Employment litigation

Wrongful termination, retaliation, discrimination, wage and hour, and Private Attorneys General Act claims, in court, in arbitration and before the National Labor Relations Board.

Civil litigation

Business, real property and contract disputes, trust and estate litigation, and personal injury, from pre-suit demand through trial and appeal.

Environmental and administrative law

Agency proceedings, regulatory compliance and enforcement, and administrative appeals before state boards and commissions.

Land use and CEQA

Entitlements, California Environmental Quality Act review and litigation, and real estate matters for owners and project sponsors.

Nonprofit and governance counsel

General counsel work for nonprofit organizations: board governance, fiscal sponsorship, tax exemption and program partnerships.

Pro bono

The firm carries a substantial pro bono docket, including reentry legal services delivered through the Rebound Institute.

§ 2Attorney

Brandon A. Kline, Principal

Brandon Kline founded the firm, originally as Kline Law Group, to bring large-firm preparation to clients who rarely get it. He practices employment and civil litigation alongside environmental, administrative and land use law.

He previously served as a Deputy Attorney General with the California Department of Justice. He holds an adjunct professor appointment at the UC Davis School of Law and was named a Presidential Leadership Scholar in 2016.
